Repaint this map of a hot southern land as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. It is drawn on two long scrolls, one above the other, on a grey stone wall; keep both scrolls, their wooden rollers and gold finials, and the stone behind them.
The jungle is dense, lush, humid green, the palms drawn across it fleshed out into thick tropical growth, only on the green ground where the map draws them. The plains are bright open grassland. The hills are warm sandy-gold rolling ground, and the Shada, Southern Shada, Hulhuk, Shuri and Koltain ranges are shaded, three-dimensional red-brown rocky mountains with deep shadow, never snow-capped. The Sadi Desert along the bottom edge is a strip of bright golden sand, never green. The Serpent Swamp and the Gurlu Marshes are murky lilac-pink marsh with reeds and pools.
The sea keeps the map's own look: deep blue in the gulfs, straits and along every coast, fading to a soft pale-blue edge and then pale open water, never all one deep teal. The rivers are winding blue lines. The roads stay white and the tracks dashed, exactly where they are, and every small house, castle and tower stays as drawn.
Keep the compass rose with its N, the knotted compass with its four letters, the scale with its numbers, the title's calligraphy and the key scroll with its gold frame, swatches and names, each in its place with its own colours and every word and number in it legible, enriched with finer detail. Add finer detail in the existing scrolls, stone and key.
No snow, no pine woods or fir trees outside the key's swatch, no ships or sea creatures, no new castles or towns.
