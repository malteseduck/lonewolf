Repaint this map of Ghatan and the surrounding territories, a harsh land on the edge of the Darklands, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Each region keeps its own colour. Naogizaga across the north is a dead grey wasteland of ash, cinders and barren rock, with no grass or trees. Ghatan and Skaror are burnt orange and rust-brown hill country, dry and harsh, never green meadow. The Aklajak, Zandzordak, Nadulritzaga, Tegaarn and Eru ranges are shaded, three-dimensional dark brown rocky peaks, and the volcanoes at Kokozritzaga glow with red fire and trail dark smoke. The dead trees near Helkona are a petrified forest of bare, twisted grey trunks. The Hammerlands and the Nogod, Moggador, Akamazim and Taintor forests are dense, individually painted dark green pine and fir woods over yellow-green plains. The Hellswamp in the west is a dark, sickly blue-green swamp of stagnant channels, mud flats and reeds, and the rivers Helnen, Ghat, Torg and Brol are murky blue. Keep the grey wasteland grey and the hills orange as on the original. Keep the gnarled tree-root frame. Add finer detail in the existing frame, banners, compass rose, scale and key.
