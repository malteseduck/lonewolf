Repaint this map of southern Sommerlund and the borderlands toward the desert of Vassagonia as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. The land changes from north to south and each region keeps its own colour. The green west and the hills around Ruanon, Quarlen and Kadan are soft green plains and rolling hills, with the round trees drawn there painted as dense broadleaf woods and the pointed ones as dark pines. The Durncrag Mountains, the Maaken Range, the Vakar Mountains and the ranges along the western edge are shaded, three-dimensional brown rocky peaks, and the Maakengorge is a deep, dark, sheer-sided chasm cutting through them. The Wildlands in the north centre are a pale cream waste of dry scrub and bare earth, not green farmland. The Maakenmire Swamp and the Mudgraves are murky teal-green marsh with dark pools and reeds, and the Bone Hills are bleak grey-green. The Dry Main in the south around Casiorn is golden-yellow desert sand with faint wind ripples, and the tree symbol beside it is an oasis of date palms; there is no grass or forest on the desert, so keep it sandy yellow as on the original. The sea to the east and the Gulf of Durenor are deep blue water with soft depth and wave texture, with the Lakuri Islands and Samiz as small sandy islands. The palm fronds in the corners are part of the frame. Add finer detail in the existing frame, heraldic panels, compass rose and key.
