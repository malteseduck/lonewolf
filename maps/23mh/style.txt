Repaint this map of Central Southern Magnamund, two long parchment sheets pinned to weathered wooden boards, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration. The plains are warm pale green grassland and the hills soft rolling tan ground with shaded rises. The mountains are shaded, three-dimensional orange-brown rocky ranges with deep shadow. The forests are dense, individually painted dark green conifers, only where the map draws trees; the Great Forest of Kelderwood keeps its bare pale clearing in the middle, with no trees in it. The Doomlands of Naaros are a bare grey-white waste with no plant on it, split by the one glowing red and orange chasm as drawn. The Northern Sadi Desert is pale yellow sand and dunes, never green. The rivers and lakes are clear blue. The sea to the west and the inlet at Tentarias is a pale wash deepening to blue along the shores, never tropical turquoise, and the islands stay small and green and tan. Keep the wooden boards, the torn sheet edges, the red and gold border, the red arrow, the brass compass, the title, the key with its swatches and the scale exactly as on the original. No ships, sea creatures, roads or walls on the land or water, no trees on the desert or the waste, and no woodcutters' stumps. Add finer detail in the existing border, boards and key.
