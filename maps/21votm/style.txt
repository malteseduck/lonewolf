Repaint this map of the coastal route between Sommerlund and Dessi, drawn as two unrolled parchment scrolls one above the other, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. In the upper scroll Sommerlund and Durenor are soft green plains and rolling hills, and their scattered conifers are dense, individually painted dark green pine woods. The Wildlands between them are pale sandy waste, open and bare, with only the few trees drawn there. The Kaltersee, the Holmgulf and the Gulf of Durenor are cool grey-blue water with soft depth and wave texture, and the Kirlundin and Lakuri islands stay small green islands. In the lower scroll Vassagonia's desert is warm orange and ochre sand with drifting light and shadow, crossed by pale dashed tracks, and the Dahir, Koneshi and Masourn ranges are continuous, sculpted brown rocky mountains with sunlit faces and deep shadow, not rows of symbols. The Bavari Hills are soft shaded rises. Dessi in the southeast is green jungle of individually painted palms around its mountains, and the coasts there are lush. The sea in both scrolls is a clear mid-blue with paler shallows along every coast. Rivers are winding blue lines. Draw each town, castle, tower and domed palace exactly as on the original, with the same outline, size, place and colours: its red roofs, flags and pennants, pale walls, gates and windows all kept. Then enrich it as a small, crisp, finely painted building: cut stonework, roof tiles, sharp edges, sunlit walls and soft shadow, never a blurred blob. Keep the key with its swatches and names, the title, the scale bar, the compass rose, both sailing ships, the sun ornament at the lower left, the wooden scroll rollers and the striped border, all as sharp as the original, adding finer detail in them only. Clear ground behind a name means no tree or hill under its letters, and the painting runs right up to them: no pale patch, glow, outline or plate behind any name. Add no forest to the desert or the Wildlands, and no ships, buildings or castles that are not drawn.
