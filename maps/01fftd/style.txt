Repaint this map of the Lastlands, the temperate northern kingdoms of Sommerlund and Durenor around a cold northern sea, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Sommerlund in the west and Durenor in the east are soft green plains and gentle hills, the scattered trees drawn across them are dense, individually painted dark green broadleaf and pine woods, and the rivers are thin winding blue lines. The Durncrag Mountains along the western edge and the ring of peaks around Hammerdal are shaded, three-dimensional brown rocky ranges with grey crags. The Wildlands across the south between Holmgard and Port Bax are a pale ochre waste: dry, rough, sparsely grassed scrubland and bare earth, not green farmland and not desert sand; keep it the pale tan of the original with only a faint green edge where it meets the forests. The Kaltersee, the Holmgulf and the Gulf of Durenor are cool grey-blue water with soft depth and wave texture, never tropical turquoise, and the Kirlundin Isles stay small green islands as drawn. Keep the ships, the ragged burnt parchment edges and the striped border as on the original. Add finer detail in the existing frame, heraldic panels, compass rose and key.
