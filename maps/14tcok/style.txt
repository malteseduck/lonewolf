Repaint this map of Sommerlund and the Darklands as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. The mountains are shaded, three-dimensional rocky ranges. The hills are soft rolling ground with shaded rises. The water keeps the colour the map gives it, with soft depth and wave texture, never tropical turquoise. Keep every town, castle and tower drawn exactly where it is. Add finer detail in the existing frame, panels and key. Most of the map is the Darklands: open dead grey wasteland of ash and cinders with sandy tan and ochre mountains and hills, no grass or farmland. The volcanoes are dark cones, red-hot at their craters, and Helgedad lies by a chasm of glowing orange lava. The northwest is white tundra and broken pack ice in pale cold water. Sommerlund in the east, the Reclaimed Wasteland and a patch of forest are green; the swamp by Eshnar is purple. The Maakengorge is one narrow red-brown ravine exactly where it is drawn: paint no other chasm. Keep the farming and fortress scenes in the right and left borders. No ships and no compass rose: add none.
