The first picture is a printed fantasy map and the second is the same map repainted by hand. Repaint the third picture, a different map, the way the second was painted. Take only the way of painting, not the example's colours or ornament. Be bold: the result must look richly painted, not like the printed original. Ink every outline dark. Mass the trees into deep, dark woods with lit edges, each tree given definition. Vary the meadows from light to deep green with painted shading. Sculpt each mountain with a lit face and a deep shadow side. Give rivers and lakes depth. Enrich the frame, shields, panels and compass with shading and fine detail, in their own colours. Keep every place, line, word and number exactly where and as it is, and add no features.
