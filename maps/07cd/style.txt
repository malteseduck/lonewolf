Repaint this map of the Magiocracy of Dessi, a hot land running from desert in the north to tropical jungle in the south, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Each region keeps its own colour. The Dry Main and the Vassagonian border in the north are golden-yellow desert sand with soft dunes, and the Bavari Hills are sun-baked ochre and tan desert hills, dry and treeless, never green. The Xulun, Vijuni, Cargos and Mangnos ranges and Heaven's Chair Mountain are shaded, three-dimensional red-brown bare rocky peaks. The Kakush Plains and the open land of Dessi are warm green-gold grassland. The trees drawn across the south are tropical: dense steaming jungle of palms, broad-leaved trees and creepers in rich deep greens, never temperate oak or pine woods; this includes the Boari Jungle and the land around Elzian and Lon. The Serpent Swamp and the Bita Swamp are dark green, waterlogged jungle swamp with murky channels. The rivers are winding brown-green jungle rivers, and the Bay of Sharks, the Bay of Lon and the sea around the coast are warm clear blue water with turquoise shallows. Add finer detail in the existing frame, title panel, compass rose and key.
