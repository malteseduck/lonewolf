Repaint this map of Central Northern Magnamund, the crowded free lands south of the Darklands, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. The mountains across the north and centre are shaded, three-dimensional red-brown rocky ranges with grey crags and deep shadow. The small arc symbols are low tan rolling hills. The south is soft green plains and gentle hills, and the round trees and conifers drawn across them are dense, individually painted dark green woods. The Wildlands in the northeast are a bare pale grey waste, and the Dry Main in the east is pale golden scrubland. The marsh by the Danarg is a pink and purple swamp of mud, reeds and pools. The lakes, the rivers and the sea are deep blue water with soft depth and shaded banks. The Maakengorge is one narrow brown ravine on the line the original draws: paint no other chasm, and the roads stay pale roads. Keep the towns small, as the map is crowded. Keep the war banner, the royal arms, each shield with its own colours and device, the scrolls beneath them, the scale and the compass in the corner as on the original. Add finer detail in the existing title scroll, leather-coloured panel with its ruled lines, shields, scale and key.
