Repaint this map of the Emberlands, a made-up island of ash-grey mountains, dark forests and a pale southern waste, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. The lowlands are soft green plains, the middle of the island is olive hill country under shaded, three-dimensional brown mountain ranges with grey crags, and the south, the Cinder Wastes, stays a pale tan waste with only a faint green edge where it meets the forests. The sea keeps the colour the map gives it, a clear blue with soft depth and wave texture, never tropical turquoise. Keep the three banners, the key, the scroll title, the ships and the compass rose as drawn, and add finer detail in the existing frame, panels and key.
