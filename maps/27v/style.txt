Repaint this map of the Autocracy of Bhanar and the Chai Borderlands as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment, a wide open plain walled by mountains on its eastern side. Most of Bhanar is open grassland: warm yellow-green steppe with soft texture, never desert sand and never farmland.
The hills are low shaded rises of olive and tan grass. The mountains in the north and east are continuous sculpted, shaded three-dimensional ranges of grey-brown rock, bare, never snow-capped, never rows of identical symbols. The coniferous forests are dense dark green firs on their darker ground, only where the map draws them. The swamp in the south-east is dull olive marsh with reeds and small dark pools, spreading among the river mouths. The sea and the gulf are a deep cold grey-blue with soft depth and wave texture, never tropical turquoise; the rivers are narrow and blue. The roads stay pale and the tracks dashed exactly where they are, and every town, fort and castle stays as drawn.
Keep, each in its place with its own colours and every word and number in it legible, enriched with finer detail: the title panel with its curled ends, the compass with its face at the top, the scale panel with its long blade and its numbers, and the key with its swatches and names. Add finer detail in the existing frame: the tiered roof, the tiger on its pillar and the armoured warrior down the left side, and the knotted column down the right.
No snow, no jungle, palms or leafy woods, no rice fields or farmland, no ships or creatures, no armies, no new towns, castles, towers, bridges, temples or ruins.
