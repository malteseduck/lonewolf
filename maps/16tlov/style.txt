Repaint this map of the Maakengorge and its lands as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment, with the depth and sculpted relief of a painted bird's-eye view, not a chart of symbols. The original draws its mountains as rows of small identical symbols: do not copy them one by one or return them recoloured. Paint every range, in the same red-brown throughout, as continuous, sculpted, three-dimensional rock with jagged ridgelines, grey crags and deep shadow. The small arc symbols are low rolling hills. Each colour of ground stays where the original has it: the grey ground is grey-lilac wasteland, the green ground soft green plains and gentle hills. Trees are dense, individually painted woods on the spots where they are drawn. Rivers and lakes are dark blue water with depth. Add finer detail in the existing border, title scroll and key.
