Repaint this map of a frozen arctic land as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Everything north of the line of broken pack ice is snow and ice: the land is white snowfields with soft blue-grey shading, the ice shelves and the glacier are pale blue-green ice, and the mountain ranges are shaded, three-dimensional rocky peaks dusted with snow. There is no grass, no forest, no farmland and no open water anywhere north of the pack ice: keep it white and icy as on the original. South of the pack ice the sea is cold, pale grey-blue water with drifting ice, never deep blue or teal; only the small islands and coasts along the bottom edge stay green as drawn. Add finer detail in the existing frame, heraldic panels, compass rose and key.
