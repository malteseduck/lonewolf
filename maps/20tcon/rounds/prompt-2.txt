The first picture is a printed fantasy map and the second is the same map repainted by hand. Repaint the third picture, a different map, the way the second was painted. Take only the way of painting, not the example's colours or ornament. Be bold: the result must look richly painted, not like the printed original, yet as light overall as the printed original and its colours as clear: the depth comes from shading, light against shadow, not from darkening. Ink every outline crisply. Paint the woods with each tree given definition, a sunlit crown and a shaded side. Vary the meadows from light to rich green with painted shading. Sculpt each mountain with a sunlit face and a shadow side. Give rivers and lakes depth. Enrich the frame, shields, panels and compass with shading and fine detail, in their own colours. Keep every place, line, word and number exactly where and as it is, and add no features.
