Repaint this map of Northern Magnamund, a whole continent of many lands, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment, with the depth and sculpted relief of a painted bird's-eye view, not a chart of symbols: rich, varied colour, soft shadows and texture everywhere, every region still in the colour the key gives it. The original draws its mountains, hills and forests as rows of small identical symbols: do not copy those symbols one by one. Paint each mountain range, the Durncrag, Dahir and Chah ranges, the Boden Peaks and Gt. Bor Range among them, as continuous, sculpted orange-brown and ochre rock: jagged ridgelines, peaks of different heights, shadowed slopes on the right, sunlit faces on the left, valleys and passes between them, covering the same ground as the symbols. Paint the hills as soft rolling tan ground with shaded rises, not rows of bumps, and the forests as dense masses of individually painted green trees with darker shadowed edges, in the same shapes as on the original. The plains are warm cream-yellow grassland with faint texture. The far north is white ice and broken pack ice with soft blue-grey shadows, and the Kaltersee and the seas are cold grey-blue water with soft waves near the shores and darker depths offshore. The Darklands around Naogizaga and Helgedad are a dead grey-green wasteland of ash, cinders and barren rock, with no grass or trees; the volcanoes are dark cones, red-hot at their craters, with dark smoke, and the red chasms are glowing fiery rifts. The swamps, the Hellswamp and the Danarg, are murky lilac-grey marsh with reeds and pools. The Dry Main between Casiorn and the Dahir Mountains is golden-yellow desert sand with soft dunes, never green. The lakes are clear deep blue. Add finer detail in the existing chequered frame, title scroll, compass rose and key.
