Repaint this map of Northern Magnamund, a whole continent of many lands, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment, keeping its light, delicate watercolour palette and every region in the colour the key gives it. The far north is white ice and pack ice with soft blue-grey shading, and the Kaltersee and the northern seas are pale cold grey-blue. The Darklands around Naogizaga and Helgedad are a dead grey-green wasteland of ash and barren rock, with the volcanoes glowing red and the red chasms as fiery rifts. The mountains everywhere, the Durncrag, Dahir and Chah ranges, the Boden Peaks and Gt. Bor Range among them, are shaded, three-dimensional orange-brown rocky peaks, and the hills are low tan rises. The plains are pale cream-yellow, and the forests are dense, individually painted green woods. The swamps, the Hellswamp and the Danarg, are lilac-grey marsh with reeds. The yellow Dry Main between Casiorn and the Dahir Mountains is bright golden-yellow desert sand, never green. The lakes are clear blue. Keep the land pale and light rather than dark and saturated. Add finer detail in the existing chequered frame, title scroll, compass rose and key.
