Repaint this map of Ixia and the Hardlands, a bleak frozen northern wilderness, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Almost the whole land is the Hardlands: cold grey-brown steppe of dust, scree and frost-bitten ground, never green grassland, and the small arc symbols scattered across it are low dry rocky hills. The Ezar Mountains, the Shegtar Peaks and the ranges inland are shaded, three-dimensional pale sandy rock with grey crags, snow on the highest peaks and deep shadow. The scattered conifers drawn across the steppe are dark, dense, individually painted pine woods. The far north around Xaagon and Gourizaga is white-blue ice and tundra with drifts and cold shadow, and each snow cross is a field of broken pack ice. The Tozaz Sea, the Gulf of Konkor and Lake Ghargon are deep dark blue cold water with soft depth, wave texture and pale ice-grey shallows along the coasts. The rivers are thin dark winding lines. Keep the sea creature, the ice spires and the undead army in the right border, the storm and meteor along the top, and the torn parchment edge as on the original. Add finer detail in the existing storm, borders, title panel, scale and key.
