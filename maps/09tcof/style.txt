Repaint this map of the Republic of Anari and its neighbours as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. The west and centre, Slovia, the Slovarian Plain and Lourden, are open soft green farmland plains, and the round trees drawn across Slovia, Anari and the Daroga Forest are dense, individually painted broadleaf woods in deep green. The east and south toward Firalond, the Kakush Uplands and Bidah are warm ochre and tan rolling hills, drier and browner than the plains. The Chah Mountains, the Southern Chah Range and the Pyllanti Mountains are shaded, three-dimensional brown rocky peaks. The yellow patches in the north-east corner and beyond the Southern Chah Range are dry desert sand with faint dunes, never green. Lake Adon and the rivers Chah, Churdas, Suen and Gaidon are clear blue water with depth. The dashed lines are roads and the Gold Trail. Add finer detail in the existing frame of carved leaves, heraldic shields and banners, compass rose, scale and key.
