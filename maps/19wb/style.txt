Repaint this map of Sommerlund and the Wildlands, a cool northern kingdom on a cold sea, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Sommerlund is soft green plains and farmland and gentle hills, and the scattered trees drawn across it are dense, individually painted dark green pine woods. The Durncrag Mountains in the west and the ranges in the south are shaded, three-dimensional brown rocky mountains with grey crags and deep shadow. The Wildlands in the east are a bare pale grey-white waste of dust and cracked ground, with no trees. The Kaltersee, the Holmgulf and the Gulf of Durenor are cool grey-blue water with soft depth and wave texture, never tropical turquoise, and the Kirlundin Isles stay small green islands. The rivers are winding blue lines. The red and white roads and tracks, and every small number beside them, stay exactly where they are and read exactly as drawn. Keep the key scroll with its numbers, names and small diamond marks, the open book's pages and fold, the border, and the North, South, East and West panels as on the original. Add finer detail in the existing border, panels, title and key scroll.
