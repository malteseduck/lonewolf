Repaint this map of the Stornlands, a temperate river country of small warring kingdoms, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. The land is lush green plains and meadows, and the round trees scattered across it are dense, individually painted broadleaf woods and copses in deep green. The River Storn, the Quall, the Eloni and the Suen are winding blue rivers with depth and texture. The great mountain wall across the north and the Varettian Hills, the Slovarian Range and the scattered hill clusters are shaded, three-dimensional warm brown and ochre rocky peaks. The Maakengorge along the north is a deep, dark, sheer-sided chasm with the Falls of Lamenta and Doom Falls pouring into it, and Lake Kazonara and Lake Vorndarol are dark mountain lakes. Keep the plains green and temperate: no desert, no snowfields. Add finer detail in the existing frame, heraldic shields and banners, patterned border, compass rose and scale.
