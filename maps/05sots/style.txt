Repaint this map of the Empire of Vassagonia, a vast hot desert realm, as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Almost the whole land is desert: warm golden-yellow and ochre sand with soft wind-rippled dunes, sun-baked hard pan and pale dust, and the small arc symbols scattered across it are low dry sandy hills and dune crests, never grassy hills. The Dahir, Kabar, Chah and Koneshi ranges and Mount Dhanamet are shaded, three-dimensional bare red-brown rock with jagged sun-scorched spires and deep shadow, with no snow and no forest on them. Each palm symbol is a small oasis: a cluster of date palms around a pool of water in the sand. Lake Inrahim is a white salt lake with a pale crusted shore. Only the thin coastal strip in the far northwest around Kuchek, Ferufezan and Lohn is green as drawn; there is no green grassland, meadow, farmland or woodland anywhere else, so keep the empire sandy yellow as on the original. The roads are pale caravan tracks across the sand. The sea along the north and east coasts is bright warm blue water with sandy shallows, and the Khordaim Islands and Lyam are small sandy islands. Keep the ship and the scroll rollers at the corners. Add finer detail in the existing scroll frame, title panel, lion-faced compass rose, scale and key.
