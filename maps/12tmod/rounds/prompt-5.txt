Repaint this map of Sommerlund and the Darklands as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment, with the depth and sculpted relief of a painted bird's-eye view, not a chart of symbols. The original draws its mountains and hills as rows of small identical triangle and dome symbols: do not copy those symbols one by one. Paint each range as continuous, sculpted mountains: jagged ridgelines, peaks of different heights and shapes, steep shadowed slopes on the right, sunlit faces on the left, valleys and passes between them, covering the same ground as the symbols. Paint the hills as soft rolling ground with shaded rises, not rows of bumps. Most of the map is the Darklands: the open ground around Naogizaga, Kaag and Aarnak is a dead grey wasteland of ash and cinders, barren rock and dark scree, and no grass, trees or farmland. The Gourdanak, Durncrag and Kagazitzaga ranges and the other Darkland mountains are sandy tan and ochre rock. The volcanoes are taller dark cones, red-hot at their craters, with dark smoke trailing from them. Helgedad sits in a fiery chasm of glowing orange lava. The northwest beyond Orgadtezna is frozen white tundra and broken pack ice, and the Kaltersee and the Gulf of Helenag are pale cold grey-blue water with soft waves near the shore. Only Sommerlund in the east, around Toran, Holmgard and the Wildlands, is green: soft green plains with dense, individually painted dark green forests. The swamp near Temel is a murky purple-brown marsh with reeds and pools, and Lake Kazonara and Lake Vorndarol are dark blue mountain lakes. The Maakengorge is one narrow ravine, painted exactly where the original draws it as a thin brown line running from Magador east towards Ruanon, just north of L. Vorndarol: keep it that thin, that long and on that line, a single dark gash with no branches. Paint no other chasm, crack or ravine anywhere on the map, and do not turn roads or rivers into ravines.
