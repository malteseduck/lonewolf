Repaint this map of Sommerlund and the Darklands as a richly detailed hand-painted fantasy illustration on aged parchment. Most of the map is the Darklands: the open ground around Naogizaga, Kaag and Aarnak is a dead grey wasteland of ash, cinders and barren rock, with no grass, trees or farmland. The mountains and hills across the Darklands, the Gourdanak, Durncrag and Kagazitzaga ranges among them, are shaded, three-dimensional sandy tan and ochre rocky peaks and bare rounded hills, and the volcano peaks glow red at their tips with dark smoke. Helgedad sits in a fiery chasm of glowing orange lava. The northwest beyond Orgadtezna is frozen white tundra and broken pack ice, and the Kaltersee and the Gulf of Helenag are pale cold grey-blue water. Only Sommerlund in the east, around Toran, Holmgard and the Wildlands, is green: soft green plains with dense, individually painted dark green forests. The swamp near Temel is a murky purple-brown marsh, and Lake Kazonara and Lake Vorndarol are dark blue mountain lakes beside the black chasm of the Maakengorge. Keep the Darklands grey and the hills tan as on the original. Add finer detail in the existing border of battling armies, title ribbons, north arrow, scale and key.
