ABOUT THIS SET
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

Originally etched by Georgio Widman in 1680 for Giovanni Giacomo de Rossi's atlas published in 1692. Converted to brushes by K. M. Alexander in 2019. Distributed as PNGs in 2026. (Seriously, it took that long?)

The symbols in this set are pulled from the 1680 Alta Lombardia map of Northern Italy, engraved by Georgio Widman for Giovanni Giacomo de Rossi’s atlas published in 1692. It’s a solid set with a heeeavy focus on mountains (over one-hundred!) as well as a wide variety of forts, villages, cities, and towns.

More details on this set and other brushes can be found at: kmalexander.com

Thanks for downloading this brush set, and I hope you find it useful.
