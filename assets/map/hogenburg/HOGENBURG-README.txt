Hogenburg: A Free 16th Century Urban Cartography Brush Set for Fantasy City Maps (PNG Pack)

Engraved by Frans Hogenburg for "Ierusalem", Civitates Orbis Terrarum vol. IV (16th century).
Converted to brushes and PNGs by K. M. Alexander (#NoBadMaps, kmalexander.com).
Source: https://kmalexander.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/hogenburg-urban-cartography-png-pack.zip

Only the symbols the town plans use are copied here (the whole pack waits, git-ignored, in
data/reference/symbol-packs/). hogenburg.json lists them by kind; maps.js tints them to the
maps' sepia ink at load, as it does the Widman stamps.

  fountain   Fountain_1, Fountain_3, Fountain_13      in the squares
  tomb       Sepulchres_1 to Sepulchres_9            the Graveyard's ground, Ragadorn's Tomb Square
  monument   Monuments_2                             a stone signpost or memorial on a square
