Reading the player's icons...

Every icon the player draws apart from the books' own items (those are on each book's item sheet): the disciplines, the tabs and gauges, the fight's medallions, the icons an item gets by its kind, and the engine's own items, which every book shares. Each shows as a medallion and large. Change icon... picks another, from the icons here or from game-icons.net (fetched into assets/icons/ and credited in its README); it is written to glyphs.js, or itemglyphs.js for an engine item, and changes in every book at once. The compass rose and the plates' ornaments are drawn in code, not icons.
