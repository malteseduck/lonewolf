Reading the item tables...

Every item the book's table defines, then the ones from elsewhere (the engine's, another book's) its sections name, each with its icon as the inventory draws it and large. A card ringed in brown shows its kind's default icon: none is chosen for it. Change icon... picks another, from the icons here or from game-icons.net (fetched into assets/icons/ and credited in its README); the item's glyph is rewritten in its table, or in itemglyphs.js for the engine's own items, which every book shares.
