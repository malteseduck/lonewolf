# 0010. Plain human enemies get varied pictures

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-09

## Context

The broad picture-library entries for soldiers, guards and robbers would show the same picture in
every such fight of a book full of them.

## Decision

- Each guard, soldier or bandit kind in a book gets its own entry: a different illustration, or a
  different figure cropped from one, from the book's non-fight pictures or other books' plain humans.
- The broad entries are a last resort.
- Another book's special creature or set-piece fight is never borrowed, unless it is the very same
  creature (spec 6.5).

## Consequences

The procedure is in `docs/reference/items.md`; Book 4 is the worked example.
