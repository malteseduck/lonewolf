# 0016. Book 2's rule readings

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01

## Context

Some of Book 2's rules can be read more than one way. The owner settled them.

## Decision

- Hunting does not excuse a Meal anywhere in the Wildlands, Ragadorn and its coast road included
  (sections 32, 37, 127, 148, 346).
- Documents and the Gold Ring are Special Items; the Healing Potion, Potion of Orange Liquid, Fur
  Blanket and Rope are Backpack Items.
- "Some Laumspur" (36) is the herb or any Laumspur potion, and one is used up.
- "Ever been given" (328) counts a Pendant since lost.
- "No pass" (246) closes when he holds either pass.

## Consequences

These are applied in Book 2's item table and `docs/reference/rules-books.md`. A later book with a
similar case decides it in its own ADR or its own rules-books section, not by analogy.
