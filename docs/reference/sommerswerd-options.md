# Sommerswerd: options for taming it

Not built and not decided: ideas kept for the owner to revisit. The engine still applies the books' rule
(`rules-books.md`, Book 2: +8 COMBAT SKILL, double ENDURANCE from undead), with the Combat Ratio capped
at +10 (ruling R1). Whatever is chosen would be a house rule: a new ruling, with `rules-books.md` and
`rules-engine.md` updated.

## The problem

The Sommerswerd's +8 stacks with sword mastery (+2 Weaponskill, +3 or +4 Weaponmastery, +5 Grand
Weaponmastery), while an ordinary weapon has no bonus of its own and tops out at +5. `LW.armedWith`
always fights with whichever adds most, so once found it is the only melee weapon that matters, and
early fights become a formality.

## Recommended: Unleash the Sun, with Awakening

An ordinary blade most of the time, whose power the player chooses when to release. All numbers are a
first guess, to be tuned.

- **Always on**: +3 COMBAT SKILL plus sword mastery; it still absorbs magic.
- **Unleashed**: a button in the fight, like Psi-surge, gives the full +8 and double damage to undead
  for 3 rounds.
- **Charges**: holds up to 3, and regains one every 15 sections passed through.
- **Awakening**: the always-on bonus grows by book (+3 in Book 2, rising towards +8) while the unleashed
  bonus stays +8, which answers the later-book risk below.
- **Book text wins**: a section that bars the sword or adds to its bonus stands as written.

## The other options

| Idea | How it works | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Unleash alone | The charges above, with a fixed always-on bonus | Later books may be too hard |
| Awakening alone | +3 in Book 2, rising with each book to +8 | Simple and needs no button, but adds no decisions in a fight |
| Sunlight | Full power by day and in the open, weak underground or at night | Time of day cannot be read reliably from the text, so the item tables or the player would mark it, as with the Kai Weapons (ADR 0047) |
| Costs the wielder | Full power costs ENDURANCE each round, like Psi-surge | Easy to build, but feels like a penalty rather than a special attack |
| Overheats | Each round at full power builds heat; too much forces it down to +0 | Most dramatic, but adds bookkeeping to every fight |

## To settle when revisiting

- **Later books**: foes in the Grand Master books may be tuned on the assumption of the full +8. Test
  with Node play-throughs (ADR 0036) before settling numbers.
- **Ruling R1**: the +10 cap can stay, limiting how far an unleashed fight runs away; it may matter less
  once the sword is weaker.
- **By the book**: whether to offer an option to play with the books' rule.
